## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Olist Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset** to understand customer behavior, revenue trends, customer retention, delivery experience, and customer value.

The objective was to create a **3–5 page data story** for leadership at an e-commerce startup, focusing on customer acquisition, retention, lifecycle marketing opportunities, and actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Olist has strong customer acquisition and revenue volume, but the key question is:

> **Are customers coming back, or is the business mainly dependent on first-time buyers?**

The analysis explores whether revenue growth is driven by new customer acquisition, repeat purchases, customer value, product categories, regional demand, and delivery experience.
